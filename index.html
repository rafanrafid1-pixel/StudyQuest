XP Calculation Formula

Task XP rewards are dynamic and calculated using your task positioning on the matrix, duration estimate, and upcoming deadline bonuses.

Base XP = Math.round((Urgency * Importance) / 100)
Time Bonus = 15m (1.0x), 1h (1.2x), 2h+ (1.5x)
Deadline Bonus = Due Today (+25 XP)
High Urgency (X axis) and High Importance (Y axis) tasks yield maximum XP payout upon completion.

Study Planning Guide

Use the matrix to prioritise schoolwork, then schedule revision before tests and break big projects into smaller steps.

Q1

Do First — Urgent & Important

Tasks in the top-right: high urgency (X axis ≥ 50) and high importance (Y axis ≥ 50). Examples include a test tomorrow, overdue coursework, or an assignment due very soon.

Action: Start with the next concrete step and give it focused time today.
Q2

Plan Revision — Important, Less Urgent

Tasks in the top-left: important work that is not yet urgent, such as revising for a test next month, starting coursework early, or practising a topic you find difficult.

Action: Schedule short, realistic study sessions before the deadline gets close.
Q3

Quick Actions — Urgent, Lower Importance

Tasks in the bottom-right: time-sensitive but lower-impact actions, such as confirming a school arrangement or packing something needed tomorrow.

Action: Handle quickly if possible, but do not let small tasks displace important revision or coursework.
Q4

Optional / Later — Less Urgent, Lower Importance

Tasks in the bottom-left: optional extras or low-priority tasks with no close deadline.

Action: Leave these until more important schoolwork is under control; remove them if they are no longer useful.
